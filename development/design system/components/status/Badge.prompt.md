Status & classification-tier badge — monospace, uppercase hairline pill. Use `critical` (red) only for the top severity tier.

```jsx
<Badge tone="critical" dot>CRITICAL</Badge>
<Badge tone="high">HIGH</Badge>
<Badge tone="medium">MEDIUM</Badge>
<Badge tone="low">LOW</Badge>
<Badge tone="neutral" dot>ACTIVE</Badge>
```

Tones: `critical` | `high` | `medium` | `low` | `neutral`. `dot` adds a leading status dot. Reserve `critical`/red for the highest tier or tamper states.
